## Important Limitations

### Data Source
- This system uses **synthetic weather data** generated for demonstration purposes
- Real meteorological data from professional weather services should be used for operational forecasting
- The synthetic data does not represent actual weather patterns or climate conditions

### Model Limitations
- Models are trained on simulated data and may not generalize to real-world conditions
- No validation against actual meteorological observations
- Performance metrics are based on synthetic data only

- **Marine weather warnings**
- **Any critical weather-dependent operations**
